Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Application Name: BustryKaro (Package: com.example.bustrykaro)

Welcome to BustryKaro. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate bus routes, live bus tracking, and seamless authentication, we collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number & OTP Authentication: We collect your mobile phone number via Firebase Authentication solely to verify your account, send SMS One-Time Passwords (OTP), and authenticate your login. We do not use your phone number for unsolicited marketing.
  • Location Data (Precise & Approximate Location): With your permission, our app accesses your device's GPS location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to display nearby bus stops, calculate walking distances to bus stops, and render live bus tracking on maps.
  • Device & Usage Data: We may collect standard non-personally identifiable technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs to optimize performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To authenticate users and maintain secure user accounts.
  • To provide real-time bus tracking, route searching, and nearby stop calculations.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To improve application performance, fix bugs, and optimize user experience.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

BustryKaro integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Used for phone authentication, analytics, and security. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements (Banner & Interstitial Ads). Google AdMob may use advertising IDs to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. AdMob Privacy Policies
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Used for secure cloud sync of bus routes and schedule database updates.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS in transit) and security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. User authentication states are stored securely on your device.

5. Your Rights & Account/Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Location Access: You can enable or disable location access at any time via your Android device settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion Request: If you wish to delete your account or erase your phone number and associated data from our servers, you may submit a request at any time by emailing us at bustrykarosupport@gmail.com. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

BustryKaro is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.
